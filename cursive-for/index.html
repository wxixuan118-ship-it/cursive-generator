Does Cursive Work on Every Platform?

Unicode cursive text works across most modern platforms, but with one important distinction: @usernames (handles) on most platforms restrict to plain alphanumeric characters, while display names and bio text support Unicode cursive freely. See the individual platform pages above for compatibility details and character limit guides for each.
