Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information Cursive Text Generator (www.cursive-text-generator.net) collects when you use the site or our Chrome extension, which third-party services are involved, how cookies are used, and the choices you have.

Summary

  • The text you type into any generator on this site is processed in your browser. We never receive it.
  • We use Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity to understand how the site is used.
  • We display advertising through Google AdSense. Google and its partners may set and read cookies to serve and measure ads.
  • We do not sell personal information, and we do not run accounts, logins or newsletters.

Information We Collect

Text you generate. Every tool on this site converts text with JavaScript that runs on your device. The words you type are not transmitted to our servers, stored or logged by us.

Server logs. Like almost every website, our hosting provider processes basic technical data to deliver pages securely: IP address, browser and device type, the URL requested, the referring page and a timestamp. These logs are kept for a limited time for security and troubleshooting.

Messages you send us. If you email us, we keep the message and your email address for as long as needed to reply and to keep a record of the conversation.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. This site does not set cookies of its own for the generator to work; the cookies you will find here are set by the third-party services below. Each of them may use cookies, local storage, pixels or similar identifiers.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-L34V5ZL59H) to learn which pages are visited, roughly where visitors come from, what devices they use and how they arrived at the site. Google Analytics 4 does not log or store individual IP addresses. You can prevent Google Analytics from collecting your data on any website by installing the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Google AdSense and Advertising Cookies

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on this site. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.

  • Third-party vendors and ad networks, including Google, may place and read cookies on your browser, or use web beacons, to collect information as a result of ad serving on this website.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • How Google uses data when you use our site is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, ads are only personalised after you give consent through the consent message shown on your first visit, and you can change that choice at any time.

Microsoft Clarity

We use Microsoft Clarity to see how visitors interact with pages through heatmaps and session recordings. Clarity captures clicks, scrolling and mouse movement, along with device and browser information. Text typed into input fields is masked by default and is not recorded. Microsoft's handling of this data is described in the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Chrome Extension

The Cursive Text Generator Chrome extension converts the text you type inside its toolbar popup, on your device. It does not collect, store, transmit or sell that text or any other personal information, and it does not access your browsing history, page content, accounts or files. Copying happens only when you press a copy button, and clipboard content never leaves your computer.

How We Use Information

We use the information described above to keep the site online and secure, to understand which tools and articles are useful, to fix display problems on particular devices, to show and measure advertising that funds the site, and to answer messages you send us.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data is shared only with the service providers named on this page (Google, Microsoft and our hosting provider), each of which processes it under its own privacy policy, and when we are required to do so by law.

Data Retention

Server logs are retained by our hosting provider for a limited period. Analytics data is retained by Google Analytics for up to 14 months in identifiable form. Email correspondence is kept for as long as it is needed to handle your request.

Your Rights and Choices

  • Cookies: you can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The generators keep working without cookies.
  • Analytics: use the Google Analytics opt-out add-on linked above, or a content blocker.
  • Advertising: manage ad personalisation through Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info, linked above.
  • Access and deletion: depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or CCPA) you may have the right to ask what personal data we hold about you and to have it corrected or deleted. Because we hold almost none, this usually only concerns email correspondence. Write to us and we will respond within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

This site is a general-audience utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when our tools or the services we rely on change. The date below shows when it was last revised; material changes will be noted here.
