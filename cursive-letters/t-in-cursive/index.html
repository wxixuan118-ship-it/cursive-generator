Cursive T Stroke Order

How to Write a Capital T in Cursive

  1. Start left of centre near cap height and sweep a wide curve to the right.
  2. Bring the stroke back left into the stem.
  3. Pull the stem down to the baseline.
  4. Finish with a small flick to the right.

The capital T does not join forward, so a gap after it is correct.

How to Write a Lowercase t in Cursive

  1. Start on the baseline and rise to about three quarters of the ascender height.
  2. Come straight back down to the baseline.
  3. Exit to the right along the baseline.
  4. Add the crossbar last, just above x-height.

The t is a half-height letter. Taking it to the full ascender line makes it an l with a bar.

Cursive t vs Cursive l

Uncrossed, a t is simply a short l — same upward stroke, same return. Two things separate them: the t stops around three quarters of the ascender height while the l goes the whole way, and the t has a bar. Because the bar is added after the word is written, a forgotten crossbar genuinely does turn the letter into an l, which is why tall can end up reading as lall.

Joining t to the Next Letter

The t exits on the baseline and joins easily. The complication is the crossbar in a word with more than one t: in letter or bottle the two stems sit side by side and a single long bar drawn across both is normal and readable. Crossing them separately is also correct, just slower.

Common Mistakes with Cursive T

  • Crossing as you go. Finish the word first, then bar every t.
  • Full ascender height. That makes it an l.
  • A bar placed too high. The crossbar sits just above x-height, not at the top of the stem.
  • Looping the stem. The t stem is a narrow up-and-down, not an open loop.

Cursive T FAQ

What is the cursive T?

The cursive T is the joined-up form of the letter T. The capital is a wide top curve over a stem and does not join forward; the lowercase is a three-quarter-height stem crossed by a short bar just above x-height. Online, “cursive T” also means the copy-and-paste characters (𝒯 and 𝓉) above.

How do you write t in cursive?

In short: up from the baseline to about three quarters of the ascender, straight back down, exit right — then cross it once the word is finished. Steps and diagram are above.

When do you cross a cursive t?

After the word is complete, not as you reach the letter. In words with two t's side by side, one bar drawn across both stems is normal.

What is the difference between a cursive t and a cursive l?

Height and the crossbar. The l reaches the full ascender line and has no bar; the t stops around three quarters of the way and is crossed. An uncrossed t is read as an l.
