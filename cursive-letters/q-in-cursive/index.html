Cursive Q Stroke Order

How to Write a Capital Q in Cursive

  1. Start below cap height and sweep anticlockwise into a full oval.
  2. Close the oval at the top, as for a capital O.
  3. Cross a short tail out through the lower right of the oval.
  4. Carry the tail below the baseline to finish.

The capital Q is a capital O with a tail. If the tail does not cross the oval it reads as an O with a stray mark.

How to Write a Lowercase q in Cursive

  1. Start at x-height and draw the bowl anticlockwise down to the baseline.
  2. Come back up the right side to close it.
  3. Drop straight down past the baseline into the tail.
  4. Kick the tail out to the right rather than looping it left.

The tail kicks right and is not looped. That is the whole difference between q and g.

Cursive q vs Cursive g

Both are a bowl with a descender, so the tail carries the entire distinction. The g loops left and returns up to the baseline to join the next letter; the q drops and kicks right, and in most hands it is unlooped. If you loop a q it becomes a g, and since q is nearly always followed by u, a looped q turns quick into something closer to guick.

Joining q to the Next Letter

In English the q is followed by a u almost without exception, so in practice you only ever have to join q to one letter. The tail kicks right and rises back to the baseline to meet the u's entry stroke. Because the pair is so fixed, many writers develop a single joined qu shape rather than two separate letters.

Common Mistakes with Cursive Q

  • Looping the tail left. That is a g.
  • A tail that curves back under the bowl. It should kick outward, to the right.
  • Leaving the bowl open. An open bowl with a tail reads as a y.
  • Forgetting the capital's crossing tail. Without it the letter is an O.

Cursive Q FAQ

What is the cursive Q?

The cursive Q is the joined-up form of the letter Q. The capital is a closed oval with a short tail crossing out below it; the lowercase is a bowl at x-height with a straight tail that drops below the baseline and kicks right. Online, “cursive Q” also means the copy-and-paste characters (𝒬 and 𝓆) above.

How do you write q in cursive?

In short: bowl first, anticlockwise from x-height to the baseline and back up; then straight down past the baseline and kick the tail out to the right. Do not loop it. Steps and diagram are above.

What is the difference between a cursive q and a cursive g?

Only the tail direction. The g loops left and returns to the baseline; the q kicks right and usually has no loop at all. The bowls are drawn identically.

Does the cursive q always join to a u?

In English, almost always — q is followed by u in essentially every common word. Many writers treat qu as a single joined shape for that reason.

Can I copy and paste a cursive Q?

Yes. Note that the Double-Struck capital (ℚ) comes from the Letterlike Symbols block rather than its own block, one of seven capitals Unicode encoded there first. It pastes like any other character.
