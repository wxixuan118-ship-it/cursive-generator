How to Write A in Cursive

Uppercase A

Uppercase cursive A is a single rounded bowl closed with a downstroke, then a short exit stroke that carries into the next letter. Unlike print A it has no crossbar.

Lowercase a

Lowercase a starts at the top of the x-height, curves anticlockwise into a closed oval, then drops a straight downstroke on the right that becomes the join.

Cursive a vs Cursive o

Both are closed ovals. The a finishes with a straight vertical downstroke on the right side; the o finishes with a small loop at the top that hooks rightwards.

Can I copy and paste A in cursive?

Yes. Every A on this page is a Unicode character, not an image, so clicking it copies real text. Paste it into an Instagram bio, a TikTok display name, a Discord nickname, or a document and the cursive shape travels with it — no font to install.

Why does cursive A look different in each style?

Each row uses a different Unicode block. Cursive Script and Bold Cursive come from the Mathematical Script ranges, Old Style Script from Fraktur, and Double-Struck and Clean Fancy from their own blocks. They are separate character sets that happen to resemble handwriting, which is why the same letter is drawn differently in each.

Is the cursive A here a real handwriting font?

No. These are Unicode characters, not a downloadable typeface. That is why they work in fields that never let you change the font. If you need an actual font file for print or design software, see our cursive fonts page.
