Cherry Font FAQ

What is a cherry font?

Online, a cherry font means text decorated with the 🍒 cherry emoji, such as 🍒 𝓝𝓪𝓶𝓮 🍒. It is Unicode text you can copy and paste, not an installed typeface.

Is this cherry font generator free?

Can I use cherry text in my Instagram bio?

Yes. Paste it into your bio, display name or a caption. Instagram shows the 🍒 emoji and the cursive letters.

Why does the cherry look different on my friend's phone?

Emoji are drawn by each platform, so the 🍒 on iOS, Android and Windows are different illustrations of the same character.

Do cherries work in usernames?

Rarely. Most platforms restrict @usernames to letters, numbers and underscores. Use the display name, nickname or bio field instead.

Is there a text-only cherry symbol?

No. Unicode has no monochrome cherry character. If a field rejects emoji, use a ♡ or ୨୧ row and add the word cherry in letters.
