Sunflower Font FAQ

What is a sunflower font?

Online, a sunflower font means text decorated with the 🌻 sunflower emoji, such as 🌻 𝓝𝓪𝓶𝓮 🌻. It is Unicode text you can copy and paste, not an installed typeface.

Is this sunflower font generator free?

Yes. It runs in your browser, needs no account or download, and every result can be copied without limits.

Can I use sunflower text in a wedding hashtag?

Yes. Copy a row and paste it into a caption or bio. Note that the emoji is not part of the searchable hashtag itself; keep #YourHashtag in plain letters and put the 🌻 beside it.

Why does the sunflower look different on my friend's phone?

Emoji are drawn by each platform, so the 🌻 on iOS, Android and Windows are different illustrations of the same character.

Do sunflowers work in usernames?

Rarely. Most platforms restrict @usernames to letters, numbers and underscores. Use the display name, nickname or bio field instead.

Is there a text-only sunflower symbol?

No. Unicode has no monochrome sunflower. If a field rejects emoji, use a ✿ or ❀ row, which are text flowers that render everywhere.
