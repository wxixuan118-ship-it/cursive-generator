Stars and sparkles overlap but are not the same. A star font generator uses five-pointed shapes: ★ ☆ ✩ ✰ 🌟 ⭐ and the ★彡 swipe. Solid, Hollow and Emoji Stars: Which Star Font to Pick

Each star character has a job. The solid ★ (U+2605) is the heaviest and works as a bullet, a rating mark or a bold frame; next to bold cursive or Fraktur it balances the weight of the letters. The hollow ☆ (U+2606) is the same shape outlined and reads lighter, which is why it pairs well with small caps, italic and thin sans letters. The ✩ and ✰ outline stars from the Dingbats block are smaller and slightly rotated, and they are what the aesthetic ⋆｡°✩ frames are built from.

The emoji stars 🌟 (glowing star) and ⭐ (white medium star) are picture characters drawn in colour by each platform. They are ideal for captions, birthday and milestone posts and TikTok display names, but they look different on iPhone, Android and Windows, count as two characters in some length limits, and are missing from some game fonts. When a star font generator result is going into a game name, the text stars are the safer choice and the ★彡 swipe is the one players recognise.

Combining stars is where most people overdo it. One frame plus one lettering style is the sweet spot: ★ with bold cursive, ⋆｡°✩ with plain or small caps letters, ★彡 with gothic. Stacking a star frame, sparkle marks and a combining-mark style on the same line makes the text hard to read and more likely to be trimmed by a character limit, so preview the exact name and trim before you copy.
