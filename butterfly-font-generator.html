How the Text Butterfly Font Ƹ̵̡Ӝ̵̨̄Ʒ Is Built

The text butterfly is not one character. It is the letters Ʒ and Ӝ with several combining marks stacked on them: Ƹ (a reversed ezh) and Ʒ (ezh) form the two wings, Ӝ (a Cyrillic zhe with diaeresis) makes the body and antennae, and the small strokes above and through them come from combining marks that sit on the letters without taking up extra width. Because every piece is standard Unicode, the whole butterfly renders anywhere plain text does and needs no emoji font at all.

That construction is also its weakness. Apps that normalise text, strip diacritics or limit the number of combining marks per character will simplify it, so it may show as ƸӜƷ without the fine strokes. That still reads as a butterfly, just a plainer one. Usernames with strict character rules will reject it entirely, which is why the generator keeps a plain 🦋 row and cursive-only rows as fallbacks.
