What Is a Cursive Signature Generator?

A cursive signature generator converts a typed name into decorative Unicode text that looks like a handwritten signature. Unlike image-based signature makers, the output is plain text — you can paste it directly into any app, social profile, or document without downloading a file or installing a font.

Bold Cursive vs Cursive Script: Which Looks More Like a Signature?

Bold Cursive (𝓢𝓲𝓰𝓷𝓪𝓽𝓾𝓻𝓮) uses thick Unicode mathematical bold script letters. The heavier weight mimics a pen signature where ink pools in the strokes — it reads as authoritative and personal. Cursive Script (𝒮𝒾𝑔𝓃𝒶𝓉𝓊𝓇𝑒) uses thinner strokes that look more like italic handwriting — elegant but lighter. For most social and email use cases, Bold Cursive is the stronger choice.

How to Use Your Cursive Signature

Instagram bio: Type your name, select Bold Cursive, click Copy, then paste into your Instagram display name or bio field. Unicode characters render correctly on both iOS and Android.
Email footer: Paste the copied text into your email client's signature editor. The text travels with your emails as standard Unicode — no image attachment needed.
Canva: Open a design, click a text layer, and paste. Visit our Canva signature templates page for ready-made layouts that pair well with cursive text.

Signature for Names with Special Characters

The Unicode cursive alphabet covers all 26 Latin letters (A–Z, a–z) and numbers. Accented characters (é, ü, ñ) will appear in standard form. For most Western names this is not an issue — try typing your name to see the result instantly.

Cursive Signature FAQ

Can I add my cursive signature to a Google Doc?

Yes. Copy the generated text and paste it into any Google Doc, Slides, or Sheets cell. Unicode characters display correctly in Google Workspace without any font installation.

What is the difference between a cursive signature and a digital signature?

A cursive signature here is decorative Unicode text for personal expression — it is not a legally binding digital signature. For legally valid electronic signatures, use a service such as DocuSign, Adobe Sign, or HelloSign.

How do I make my name look different on different platforms?

Switch between the style options in the dropdown to compare Bold Cursive, Cursive Script, Old Style Script, and more. Each uses a different Unicode block so the visual weight and feel varies. Keep "All styles" selected to see them all at once.
