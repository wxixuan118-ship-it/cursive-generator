Frequently Asked Questions

What is Tangerine font?

Tangerine is a free Google Font designed by Toshi Omagari. It mimics pointed-pen calligraphy with thin strokes and a strong italic lean, giving it an elegant and delicate appearance.

Is Tangerine good for body text?

Tangerine works best at large sizes for display use. At small sizes its thin strokes can become hard to read. It is ideal for short headings, names, and decorative phrases.

Can I use Tangerine on Instagram?

This generator gives you Unicode cursive text with a similar thin, flowing style that you can paste directly into Instagram bios and captions without any font installation.

What is similar to Tangerine?

Similar thin calligraphic fonts include Pinyon Script, Corinthia, and Imperial Script. For a similar Unicode copy-paste experience, use the Script option in this generator.

Where do I download Tangerine?

Tangerine is available free at fonts.google.com in Regular and Bold weights.
