Frequently Asked Questions

What is Sacramento font?

Sacramento is a free Google Font by Astigmatic. It is a monoline cursive with consistent thin strokes and a long, extended flow between letters, making it ideal for minimal, airy designs.

Is Sacramento good for Instagram bios?

Yes. Sacramento is popular for minimal and aesthetic Instagram bios. This generator gives you a Unicode version you can paste directly into your bio without any font installation.

What is monoline cursive?

Monoline cursive means the strokes are all the same width, unlike calligraphic fonts that have thick and thin stroke contrast. Sacramento has this consistent thin-stroke quality for a clean, modern handwritten look.

What fonts look like Sacramento?

Similar thin cursive fonts include Tangerine, Arizonia, Clicker Script, and Mr Dafoe. All are free on Google Fonts.

Can I use Sacramento in Canva?

Yes. Sacramento is available directly in Canva as part of the Google Fonts library. Search for it in the Canva font picker.
