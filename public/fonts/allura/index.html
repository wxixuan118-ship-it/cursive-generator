Frequently Asked Questions

What is Allura font?

Allura is a free Google Font designed by Rob Leuschke. It is a calligraphic script with classical proportions, designed to look refined and elegant in both display and formal contexts.

How do I use Allura style text on Instagram?

Type your text in the generator, copy the Unicode result, and paste it directly into your Instagram bio, caption, or story text field. No font installation needed.

Is Allura suitable for wedding invitations?

Yes. Allura is popular for wedding stationery. Use the actual Google Font for print; use this generator for digital invitations, posts, and social announcements.

What is the difference between Allura and Great Vibes?

Both are elegant calligraphic scripts, but Allura has slightly more classical proportions while Great Vibes has a taller, more dramatic flow. Both are refined choices for formal text.

Where can I download Allura?

Download Allura for free at fonts.google.com. It includes regular weight and works well in Canva, Adobe, Figma, and web CSS.
