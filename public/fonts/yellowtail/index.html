Frequently Asked Questions

What is Yellowtail font?

Yellowtail is a free Google Font by Astigmatic with a bold, retro-inspired cursive script style. It has wide, expressive strokes and a vintage American lettering aesthetic.

Is Yellowtail good for logos?

Yellowtail is popular for logo lettering, especially for retro, vintage, or energetic brands. Download the actual font from Google Fonts for logo design; use this generator for social media and profile text.

Can I use Yellowtail style text on social media?

Yes. This generator produces Unicode bold cursive text with a similar bold character. Copy and paste it into Instagram, TikTok, Discord, and any Unicode-supporting platform.

What fonts are similar to Yellowtail?

Similar bold retro script fonts include Pacifico, Lobster, Permanent Marker, and Fredericka the Great. All are free on Google Fonts.

Where can I download Yellowtail?

Yellowtail is available for free at fonts.google.com in a single regular weight.
