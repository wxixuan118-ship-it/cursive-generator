Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Cursive Text Generator handles information when you use our cursive font generator and cursive text generator.

Information We Collect

Our core text generator runs in your browser. The text you type into the tool is processed locally by JavaScript and is not sent to our server by the generator itself.

Like most websites, our hosting provider may process basic technical information such as IP address, browser type, device type, requested URLs, and timestamps in order to deliver the website securely and reliably.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand aggregate site usage, such as visited pages, approximate location, device type, and traffic sources. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies to collect usage data.

Google AdSense and Advertising Cookies

We use Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, and other identifiers to serve ads, limit ad frequency, prevent fraud, measure ad performance, and personalize ads where permitted.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may place and read cookies on your browser or use web beacons as a result of ad serving on this website. You can learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at Google's partner sites policy page.

Microsoft Clarity

We use Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors interact with the site through behavioral metrics, heatmaps, and session replay. Clarity may collect interaction data, device information, and usage data through cookies or similar technologies.

Your Choices

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. You can also manage Google ad personalization through Google's ad settings and related privacy controls.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We use third-party services such as Google Analytics, Google AdSense, Microsoft Clarity, and our hosting provider to operate, measure, improve, and monetize the site.

Children

This website is a general audience utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
