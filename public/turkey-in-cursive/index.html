How to Write Turkey in Cursive

Turkey starts with a capital T. The cursive capital T is a tall stem with a wide top bar, and like the capital F it does not connect to the next letter. Write it, lift the pen, and start the u just to the right at the baseline. Keep the gap narrower than a word space.

The u is two cups joined at the baseline, and from it the pen rises into the r: a small point at the midline followed by a slight dip. Then comes the k, the most complex lowercase letter in the word. The cursive k has a tall ascender loop, then a small closed loop at the midline that kicks out into a short leg. Beginners often make the midline loop too large; keep it about the size of an e.

Finish with e-y. The e is a small loop and the y drops below the baseline into a descender loop before its exit stroke. The k is the only ascender and the y the only descender, so the word rises once in the middle and dips once at the end. Aim for the loop of the k and the loop of the y to be the same size.

Turkey or Türkiye in Cursive

In 2022 the country asked the United Nations to use its Turkish name, Türkiye, in place of Turkey, and many organisations have followed. Türkiye contains an umlauted ü, which the generator cannot convert, so it comes out as a cursive T, a plain ü, and cursive r-k-i-y-e. If you need the official spelling fully styled, write it by hand or use a font with real cursive glyphs. If you need a fully converted Unicode result, Turkey remains the practical choice.

Turkish handwriting uses the Latin alphabet, adopted in 1928, so Turkish school cursive is broadly similar to the cursive on this page. The distinctive letters are the dotted and dotless i (İ/ı) and the letters with diacritics such as ş, ç, and ğ. Only the plain letters among them will convert in the tool.

Turkey in Cursive FAQ

Does the capital T in Turkey join to the u?

No. The cursive capital T is a non-connecting capital, like the F. Write it, lift the pen, and start the u just to the right at the baseline.

How do you write the k in Turkey in cursive?

Make a tall ascender loop, then a small closed loop at the midline about the size of an e, and kick out into a short leg before joining the e. Keeping the midline loop small is the key.

Will Türkiye convert to cursive in the generator?

Only partly. The ü has no script-style equivalent, so it stays plain while the other letters convert. Turkey converts fully.

How do I avoid confusion with the bird when posting Turkey in cursive?

Add a place name such as Istanbul or Cappadocia, or a travel hashtag, alongside the cursive word. Context removes the ambiguity.
