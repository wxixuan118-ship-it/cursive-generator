How to Write India in Cursive

India starts with a capital I: a tall stroke with a small loop at the bottom left that connects into the next letter from the baseline. Move directly into the n, which has two humps and sits between the baseline and the midline.

The d is the only ascender. Form the bowl first as a closed oval, like an a, then rise into a tall stroke and come back down the same line to the baseline. In cursive the d ascender is usually a plain stroke rather than a loop, though some styles loop it. Either way it should reach the same height as the capital I.

Finish with i-a: a short single stroke and a closed oval with a short exit stroke. India has two i's, one at the start as a capital and one near the end, but only the lowercase one takes a dot. Add it after the exit stroke of the a. The word has no descenders, so it sits cleanly on the line.

India, Bharat, and Why Devanagari Has No Cursive

India's other official name is Bharat, and it converts fully in the generator. The Hindi spelling, भारत, does not: the Unicode script alphabets only cover A–Z, so Devanagari passes through unchanged. The same applies to every Indian script, from Tamil to Bengali to Gujarati.

Devanagari has no cursive in the Latin sense because its letters are already connected. Each word hangs from a continuous horizontal line, the shirorekha, which is drawn across the top of the letters. The joining that Latin cursive adds with exit strokes is built into the script itself. Indian schoolchildren do learn English cursive, however, and it remains widely taught in Indian schools, which is one reason searches for India in cursive are so common.

Where India in Cursive Fits

Independence Day on August 15 and Republic Day on January 26 are the national dates. Cursive India also appears on Diwali and Holi greeting cards, in travel captions from Rajasthan, Kerala, and Goa, on restaurant menus and spice packaging, in wedding stationery, and on geography or history project covers.

The elegant script suits greeting cards and stationery. Bold cursive is the choice for captions. The decorative style can work for a single word on a dark, jewel-toned background but is heavy for most Indian themes.

India in Cursive FAQ

How do you write the d in India in cursive?

Form the bowl first as a closed oval, then rise into a tall stroke that reaches the height of the capital I and come back down the same line to the baseline before joining the i.

Which i in India gets a dot?

Only the lowercase i near the end. The capital I at the start is a tall stroke with a small loop and takes no dot.

Can I write India in Hindi with this generator?

No. Devanagari characters pass through unchanged because the Unicode script alphabets only cover A to Z. Bharat or India in Latin letters will convert fully.

Why does Hindi not have a cursive script?

Because Devanagari letters are already joined by the shirorekha, the horizontal line drawn across the top of each word. The connection that Latin cursive adds with exit strokes is built into the script.
