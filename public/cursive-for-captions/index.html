How to Use Cursive Text

Type your text in the generator at the top, select the style, and click Copy. The output is Unicode mathematical script text — not an image or font file. Paste it directly into any app or platform field that supports Unicode. Always preview in your target app before publishing.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add cursive text to an Instagram caption?

Type or paste your cursive caption text into the caption field when posting on Instagram. Cursive Unicode text is treated as plain text, so it pastes directly. For best results, put the cursive text in the first one or two lines where it's visible before the "more" cutoff.

What cursive style works best for captions?

Thin elegant script is most readable for caption text because users are actively reading it at normal body text size. Bold cursive is better for very short, one-line captions where visual impact is the priority.

Do cursive captions work on TikTok?

Yes. TikTok video descriptions and photo post captions support Unicode cursive. You can also add cursive text to text overlay cards in TikTok Studio by pasting cursive Unicode into the text input field.

How many characters can a TikTok caption be?

TikTok captions can be up to 2,200 characters for video posts. The first 55–100 characters show before the "more" button. Put your cursive opener within the first line to ensure it's visible without expanding the caption.

Can I mix cursive and regular text in a caption?

Yes. Mixing cursive for a phrase or opener with plain text for the rest of the caption is a popular approach. It creates visual contrast that draws attention to the cursive portion while keeping the full caption readable.
