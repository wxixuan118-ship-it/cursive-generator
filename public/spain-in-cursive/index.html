How to Write Spain in Cursive

Spain starts with a capital S. The cursive capital S begins with an upward stroke, curves over into a large loop, and finishes at the baseline with a small tail. In some school styles that tail connects into the next letter; in others the pen is lifted. Either way, place the p close beside it.

The p is the only descender in the word, and it comes early. Bring the stroke up to the midline, drop it straight below the baseline, and return with a small bowl that closes at the midline before continuing into the a. Because the descender sits right next to the tall capital, Spain gives you a tall stroke and a deep stroke side by side, which is good practice for keeping both proportional.

The word ends a-i-n: a closed oval, a short single stroke, and a two-hump n, all sitting between the baseline and the midline. Dot the i after the exit stroke of the n. With only five letters, Spain is short enough to write several times in a row when practicing.

Spain, España, and the Tilde

The Spanish name for the country is España, and it contains the letter that defines the language: ñ. The generator converts plain A–Z only, so España comes out with a cursive E-s-p-a, a plain ñ, and a cursive a. For a fully styled word use Spain, or write España by hand, where the tilde is simply a small wave added above the n after the word is complete.

Common companion phrases such as Viva España and Feliz Navidad follow the same rule; anything with ñ or an accent will show that one letter in plain form. Phrases without special characters, such as Hola, Madrid, and Barcelona, convert completely. The Spanish in cursive page goes deeper into handwriting the ñ and accented vowels.

Occasions for Spain in Cursive

Fiesta Nacional de España on October 12 is the national day. Cursive Spain also appears in travel captions from Barcelona, Seville, Madrid, and the Balearic Islands, on tapas-bar menus and flamenco event flyers, in wedding stationery for Andalusian venues, and on Spanish-class project covers.

The elegant script is the natural choice for menus and stationery. Bold cursive is the safest for social captions. The decorative style suits a single word on a rustic label but can feel heavy for Mediterranean themes.

Spain in Cursive FAQ

Does the capital S in Spain join the p?

It depends on the style. Some school models finish the capital S with a tail that leads into the next letter, while others lift the pen. Both are correct; keep the p close beside the S either way.

How do you write the p in Spain in cursive?

Bring the stroke up to the midline, drop it straight below the baseline, and return with a small bowl that closes at the midline before continuing into the a. It is the only descender in the word.

Will España with an ñ convert to cursive?

Partly. The E, s, p, and both a's convert, but the ñ has no script-style equivalent and stays in plain form. Use Spain for a fully styled word.
