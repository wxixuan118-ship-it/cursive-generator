    How to Make a Good Email Username

    The generator follows a handful of formulas — the ones recruiters, friends and spam filters are used to. Knowing them helps you tweak a result or build your own address by hand.

    first.lastSarah + . + Smith → the professional standard; use it for job applications
    Initial + lastS + Smith → shorter and usually still available when first.last is taken
    Name + work / writesSarah + . + writes → says what the address is for
    hello / hi / contact + namehello + . + Sarah → friendly and professional at once; the small-business standard
    Name + brand wordSarah + . + studio / design → a business address without a domain
    Two plain wordsQuiet + Inbox → no name at all, for sign-ups and newsletters
    the + family + nounthe + Smith + family → a shared household address
    Name + meaningful numberSarah + Smith + 07 → only when the plain form is taken; a birth year in an address is a privacy risk

    For anything you will put on a CV, the address should be your name and nothing else: sarah.smith or s.smith, never sarahxo2004. Keep the fun ones for personal mail — and remember a Gmail address can never be changed, only abandoned.

    Email Username Rules — Gmail, Outlook and Recruiters

    Gmail usernames are 6 to 30 characters of letters, numbers and periods — and Gmail ignores periods, so sarah.smith, sarahsmith and s.a.r.a.h.smith all deliver to the same inbox and count as the same username when you sign up. Underscores and hyphens are not allowed. Outlook and Hotmail allow letters, numbers, periods, underscores and hyphens up to 64 characters; iCloud allows 3 to 20 with periods and underscores; Proton allows letters, numbers, periods, underscores and hyphens. None of them let you rename an existing address — you can only create a new one and forward the old — which is why an email username generator is worth using before you sign up rather than after.

    The unwritten rule is about audience. A professional address is your name and nothing else (sarah.smith, s.smith); adding a nickname, a birth year or a hobby costs credibility on a CV and leaks personal data to every list the address ends up on. A personal address can carry a word; an alias for sign-ups should carry nothing. This page keeps those three apart as separate styles. If you want the same name across social platforms, the main username generator and the Instagram username generator check the same handle formats; for a private alias that also works as a Reddit account, the Reddit username generator shares the two-word anonymous pattern; and for the shortest possible address, the short username generator keeps everything under eight characters.

    Gmail's character rules and the fact that periods are ignored are documented in Google's help article on Gmail usernames.

    Email Username Generator FAQ

    What is an email username generator?

    It is a tool that combines your name, surname or brand with curated vocabulary — professional, personal, brand, creative, anonymous, minimal, student and family words — and address patterns to produce ideas for the part before the @ that follow Gmail's and Outlook's rules. This one runs in your browser, is free, and lets you copy any result with one click.

    What are good email username ideas?

    Examples from the styles above: sarah.smith, hello.sarah, sarah.studio, sarah.writes, quiet.inbox, sarah.mail, sarah.studies and the.smith.family. Type your first name for a personal address or your surname for a professional one; leave the box blank for two-word aliases.

    What is a good professional email username?

    Your name and nothing else: first.last (sarah.smith), initial plus last (s.smith) or last.first if those are taken. No nicknames, hobbies or birth years. Pick the Professional style above and type your surname to get real options.

    What characters can an email username have?

    Letters, numbers and periods, 6 to 30 characters — and periods are ignored, so sarah.smith and sarahsmith are the same address. No underscores, hyphens, spaces or symbols. Outlook and Proton also allow underscores and hyphens.

    Can I change my email username?

    No provider lets you rename an existing address. You can create a new one and forward or import the old mail, but the old address stays. That is why it is worth generating several options and checking them on the sign-up form before you commit.

    Does this email username generator check availability?

    No. Nothing here talks to Gmail, Outlook or any provider, so it cannot know whether an address is taken. Copy the one you like into the sign-up form; Gmail and Outlook show immediately whether it is available and suggest alternatives if not.

    What is a good anonymous email username?

    Two plain words with nothing personal — quiet.inbox, grey.alias, north.relay — for sign-ups, newsletters and one-off accounts. Leave the word box blank and pick Anonymous; never add your name, birth year or city to an alias.

    Can I copy and paste the generated email usernames?

    Yes. Every result has a Copy button, Copy All Usernames copies the visible list one per line, and the heart saves an address to My Favorites in your browser. Paste the result before the @ in the sign-up form.
