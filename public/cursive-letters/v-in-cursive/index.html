What Does V Look Like in Cursive?

In cursive handwriting, the letter V takes on a flowing, connected form that differs significantly from its printed counterpart. In Cursive Script — the most traditional style — uppercase V is rendered as 𝒱 and lowercase v as 𝓋. These characters come from the Unicode Mathematical Script block, which means they can be copied and pasted into any app or platform that supports Unicode text.

In Bold Cursive, the forms become 𝓥 and 𝓿 — thicker strokes that hold up better on mobile screens, making them the preferred choice for Instagram display names and TikTok bios.

How to Write V in Cursive

Slant downward from the midline to the baseline midpoint, then back up to the midline and exit right. When practicing, slow down and pay attention to where the stroke begins relative to the midline and baseline — these reference points are what give cursive letters their consistent, flowing appearance across a line of text.

Uppercase V vs Lowercase v in Cursive

Uppercase and lowercase cursive letters often look quite different from each other. Uppercase V (𝒱) is more elaborate, with additional loops and height. Lowercase v (𝓋) is simpler and designed to connect smoothly with the letters that follow it in a word. When writing full words in cursive, lowercase letters are used most of the time — uppercase is reserved for the first letter of names, sentences, and proper nouns.
