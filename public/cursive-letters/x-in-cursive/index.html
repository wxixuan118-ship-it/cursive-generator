Cursive X Stroke Order

How to Write a Capital X in Cursive

  1. Start below cap height and draw a diagonal down to the right, ending on the baseline.
  2. Exit with a small flick.
  3. Finish the rest of the word.
  4. Return and add the second diagonal, top right to bottom left.

Like the t crossbar and the i dot, the second stroke of X is added after the word, not during it.

How to Write a Lowercase x in Cursive

  1. Start at x-height and draw a diagonal down to the right, ending on the baseline.
  2. Exit to the right so the next letter can join.
  3. Finish the word.
  4. Come back and cross with the second diagonal, top right to bottom left.

The x is one of the very few lowercase letters that cannot be written without lifting the pen.

Cursive x vs a Multiplication Sign

In handwritten notes this is a real ambiguity, especially in anything with numbers. A cursive x has a visible entry stroke on the first diagonal and an exit stroke that runs on towards the next letter; a multiplication cross has neither and sits symmetrically between two terms. If your notes mix words and arithmetic, the entry and exit strokes are what tell a reader which one you meant.

Joining x to the Next Letter

The first diagonal ends on the baseline and carries the join, so the letter after an x connects normally — the second diagonal is added later and never touches the neighbouring letters. This is the same pattern as the i dot and the t bar: finish the running stroke, then go back for the extras.

Common Mistakes with Cursive X

  • Crossing mid-word. Add the second diagonal after the word, like a t bar.
  • Joining from the wrong stroke. The join leaves from the first diagonal, not the second.
  • An off-centre cross. The two diagonals should meet near the middle of the x-height.
  • Curving the diagonals. Both strokes are straight.

Cursive X FAQ

What is the cursive X?

The cursive X is the joined-up form of the letter X: two crossing diagonals in both cases. The first runs down to the right and carries the join to the next letter; the second is added afterwards, running down to the left. Online, “cursive X” also means the copy-and-paste characters (𝒳 and 𝓍) above.

How do you write x in cursive?

In short: one diagonal down to the right, exit towards the next letter, finish the word, then go back and add the crossing diagonal. Steps and diagram are above.

Does the cursive x need a pen lift?

Yes. It is one of the few lowercase letters that cannot be completed in a single stroke, in the same family as dotting an i and crossing a t.

Why do so few words start with x?

English has very few native words beginning with x — most are borrowings from Greek, like xylophone and xenon. The letter is far more common in the middle or at the end of a word, as in axis or box.
