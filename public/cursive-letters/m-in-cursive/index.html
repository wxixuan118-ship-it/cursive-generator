How to Write M in Cursive

Uppercase M

Uppercase cursive M begins with an upstroke, then two tall arches. It is noticeably wider than most capitals, so it needs extra spacing before the next letter.

Lowercase m

Lowercase m is three downstrokes joined by two rounded humps, all sitting inside the x-height. Keep the humps the same width or it starts to read as nn.

Cursive m vs Cursive n

Purely a matter of humps: m has two, n has one. In fast handwriting a rushed m is the single most common cursive misreading.

Why does cursive M look different in each style?

Each row uses a different Unicode block. Cursive Script and Bold Cursive come from the Mathematical Script ranges, Old Style Script from Fraktur, and Double-Struck and Clean Fancy from their own blocks. They are separate character sets that happen to resemble handwriting, which is why the same letter is drawn differently in each.

Is the cursive M here a real handwriting font?

No. These are Unicode characters, not a downloadable typeface. That is why they work in fields that never let you change the font. If you need an actual font file for print or design software, see our cursive fonts page.
