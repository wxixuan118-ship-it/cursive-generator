How the Ribbon Font Banners Are Built

The ━🎀━ banner is a box-drawing heavy horizontal line (U+2501) on each side of the ribbon emoji. Box-drawing characters were designed to join edge to edge, so two of them read as one continuous line and the bow sits in the middle as if tied around it. Because the line is text, it inherits the colour of the surrounding characters, and you can lengthen it by pasting extra ━ characters if a field is wide enough.

The text ribbons come from an older tradition. ౨ৎ pairs a Telugu digit-like letter with a Bengali letter whose shapes, side by side, look like a small tied ribbon; ୨୧ uses two Odia digits the same way and became the coquette bow. Neither is meant to be a ribbon, which is exactly why they render everywhere: they are ordinary letters in fonts that ship with every operating system. The ೃ⁀➷ curl adds a Kannada vowel sign, an undertie and a heavy arrow to make a trailing tail.
