Facebook Pages, Groups, and post captions all support Unicode cursive. Type your text, copy your preferred cursive style, and paste it into your Facebook Page name, post, or About section.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use cursive in my Facebook Page name?

Yes. Facebook Pages allow Unicode cursive text in their names. Go to Page Settings → Page Info → Name, paste your cursive text, and submit the change for review. The cursive name will appear on your Page and in Facebook search.

Can I use cursive in my personal Facebook name?

Facebook's real-name policy may reject Unicode cursive characters in personal profile names, as they do not match the appearance of legal names. You can freely use cursive in post captions, comments, About sections, and status updates on your personal profile.

Does cursive text work in Facebook posts?

Yes. Unicode cursive text can be pasted directly into Facebook post text, comments, and story captions. It renders correctly in the Facebook app on iOS and Android, and in the web version.

Can I use cursive in a Facebook Group name?

Yes. Facebook Group names support Unicode cursive. The admin can edit the Group name in Group Settings and paste a cursive version. The cursive name will appear in the group header and in search results.

Does Facebook cursive text work on mobile?

Yes. Unicode cursive displays correctly in the Facebook app on both iOS and Android. The characters are rendered by each device's operating system font, so minor visual differences between iOS and Android are possible, but the text is correctly copied and displayed.
