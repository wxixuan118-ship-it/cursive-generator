How Cursive Works on Snapchat

Snapchat display names support Unicode text and can be changed freely. Snap captions and story text also support Unicode when pasted from the clipboard. Copy the cursive text from the generator above, open your phone's notes app to hold it in clipboard, then paste into your Snapchat display name or snap caption.

How do I change my Snapchat display name?

Open Snapchat → tap your Bitmoji or profile icon → tap the pencil/edit icon next to your display name → delete the old name → paste your cursive text → tap Save. The cursive display name will appear to all your friends and in the Snap Map.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I change my Snapchat username to cursive?

No. Snapchat usernames are permanent and restricted to letters, numbers, hyphens, and underscores. You can only change your display name — go to Profile → tap the edit icon next to your name → paste your cursive text.

How do I add cursive text to a Snapchat story?

Copy your cursive text from this generator. Open Snapchat and take a photo or video. Tap the T button to add text. Long-press in the text field to paste your cursive text. Adjust the size and placement, then add to your story.

Does Snapchat display cursive correctly on all phones?

Yes. Both iOS and Android support Unicode Mathematical Script characters. Your cursive display name will appear correctly on all modern Snapchat versions.

Can I use cursive in Snapchat chat messages?

Yes. Snapchat chat messages support the full Unicode range. Copy and paste cursive text directly into any chat conversation.

How many characters is the Snapchat display name limit?

Snapchat display names allow up to 75 characters. Unicode cursive characters generally count as 1 character each in Snapchat's counter.
