What Is Cursive Copy and Paste?

Cursive copy and paste means generating Unicode mathematical script characters that look like cursive handwriting and copying them directly into any app or website. Unlike traditional font files, these characters are plain text — no installation, no download, no account required. They paste correctly anywhere that supports Unicode: Instagram, TikTok, Discord, WhatsApp, Twitter/X, LinkedIn, and most web-based text fields.

Which Page Should I Use?

Use Cursive Font Copy and Paste to compare all styles side by side. Use Cursive Letters to copy individual A–Z letters from the full alphabet table. Use Cursive Signature to turn your name into a signature-style script text. Use Fancy Cursive for aesthetic words and social media content. Use Handwritten Font for personal, warm, organic-feeling text. Use Cursive Writing for full phrases, quotes, and sentences.

Does Cursive Copy and Paste Work on iPhone?

Yes. Unicode cursive text works on iPhone and iPad in all major apps — Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp, iMessage, Safari, and more. Simply copy the text from the generator and paste it using the standard iOS paste action. The cursive characters display using iOS's built-in Unicode font support.
