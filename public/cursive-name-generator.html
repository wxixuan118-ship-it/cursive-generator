Cursive Name Styles Explained

Bold Cursive (𝓞𝓵𝓲𝓿𝓲𝓪): Thick, bold script — the most readable cursive style on mobile. Best for Instagram names and TikTok display names.
Elegant Cursive (𝒪𝓁𝒾𝓋𝒾𝒶): Classic mathematical script with thin, elegant loops — the traditional cursive look.
Bold Italic (𝑶𝒍𝒊𝒗𝒊𝒂): Strong italic with bold strokes — popular for formal personal styles.
Old Gothic (𝔒𝔩𝔦𝔳𝔦𝔞): Fraktur calligraphy — old-world manuscript style, popular for tattoo name aesthetics.
Double-Struck (𝕆𝕝𝕚𝕧𝕚𝕒): Mathematical double-struck characters — distinctive double-line appearance.
Italic Script (𝑂𝑙𝑖𝑣𝑖𝑎): Clean formal italic — resembles traditional handwritten italic.
