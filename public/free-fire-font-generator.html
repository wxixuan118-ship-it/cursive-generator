Free Fire Name Rules and Character Limits

A Free Fire nickname can use most Unicode letters and symbols but is limited in length — around 12 characters total, with each frame symbol counting as one or more. Spaces are allowed but count too. Names that are refused are usually too long rather than using a banned character; drop the outer frame or shorten the base name and try again. Changing your nickname costs a Name Change Card after the first free change, so test a name here before spending one.

Free Fire Font FAQ

How do I change my name style in Free Fire?

Copy a style from this page, open Free Fire, tap your profile, edit the nickname field, paste the text and confirm. The first change is free; later changes need a Name Change Card.

What does ꧁꧂ mean in Free Fire names?

They are Balinese script characters that players use as decorative wings or a crown around a name. They have no meaning in the game — they are purely a visual frame.

How many characters can a Free Fire nickname have?

Roughly 12, including spaces and symbols. Each frame symbol counts, so a heavy frame leaves room for a short base name.

Why was my stylish name rejected?

Almost always length. Shorten the base name or remove the outer symbols. A few rare characters are also filtered; swapping to a different frame fixes it.

Are Free Fire fonts safe? Is this a mod?

Yes, they are safe. The styles are standard Unicode text that Free Fire already supports. Nothing is downloaded or installed and the game is not modified.

Can I use these for my FF guild name?

Yes. Guild names accept the same Unicode text, with their own length limit. Keep guild names shorter than player names to fit.
