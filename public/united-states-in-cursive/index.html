How to Write United States in Cursive

United begins with a capital U: two tall strokes joined by a curve at the baseline, finishing with a tail that connects into the next letter. Then n-i-t-e-d: a two-hump n, a short i, a t with a short ascender, a small looped e, and a d whose bowl is a closed oval and whose ascender reaches the height of the capital. Extend the exit stroke of the d a little further than usual to create the word space.

Lift the pen and start the capital S of States. The cursive capital S begins with an upward stroke, curves into a large loop, and finishes at the baseline. Keep it the same height as the capital U so the two words look like a pair. Then t-a-t-e-s: a short t, a closed oval a, another short t, a small e, and a pointed s with an exit stroke.

Now go back and add the marks in one pass from left to right: the dot on the i in United, then the three t-crosses. The two t's in States are close together and can share a single crossbar in most school styles, which is quicker and looks tidier. Check that both words sit on the same baseline and lean at the same slant; a two-word cursive title most often goes wrong when the second word drifts.

USA and U.S. in Cursive

The country name is more often abbreviated than written out, and the abbreviations behave differently in cursive. USA is three capitals in a row. Cursive capitals are not designed to join one another, so write each separately with a small, even gap, and keep them the same height. The generator converts USA in all three styles, and the bold version is a popular choice for profile fields where the full name would not fit.

U.S. with periods is the usual short form in running text. In handwriting, write the two capitals with a clear gap and add the periods on the baseline afterward. In the generator the periods pass through unchanged, which is fine because punctuation is never styled. The full formal name, United States of America, converts completely as well, and the lowercase of between the two capitalised words gives the phrase a natural rhythm in a cursive heading.

Where United States in Cursive Is Used

The formal name suits formal occasions: citizenship-ceremony invitations, military and veterans' event programmes, government and civics project titles, passport-style travel journals, and July 4 designs that want a more official tone than America. It also appears on flag-themed wedding stationery and on Olympic or World Cup support posts.

Two words at twelve letters need room. Use the elegant script on a single line for print, put the two words on separate lines for the decorative style, and choose bold cursive or the abbreviation USA for captions and small profile fields.

United States in Cursive FAQ

How wide should the space be between United and States in cursive?

About the width of one lowercase letter. Extend the exit stroke of the d in United, lift the pen, and start the capital S after a gap that is wider than a join but narrower than a printed space.

Can the two t's in States share one crossbar?

Yes. In most school cursive styles two t's that sit close together can share a single crossbar, which is quicker and looks tidier than two separate strokes.

How do you write USA in cursive?

As three separate capitals with small, even gaps and the same height. Cursive capitals are not designed to join one another. The generator converts USA in all three styles.

Does the capital U in United connect to the n?

Yes. The cursive capital U finishes at the baseline with a tail that leads directly into the lowercase n.
