How to Write 5 (Five) in Cursive

Begin at the upper-right with a short horizontal or slightly-curved cap stroke going left. Without lifting the pen, curve down and to the right into the rounded belly — a clockwise arc that brings you to the baseline level. Finally, return to the top and add a cap stroke across the top of the number. In traditional cursive, the cap is drawn last.

Stroke Steps

  1. Draw the body first: starting at mid-left, curve clockwise down and right to form the rounded belly
  2. At the baseline, let the curve end slightly right of centre
  3. Return to the top and draw a short cap stroke left-to-right across the top of the belly
  4. The cap sits above the belly and slightly overhangs it on the right

5 in Print vs Cursive

Print 5 has a distinct angular top-left corner. Cursive 5 softens this into a flowing cap stroke with a rounded belly below, giving it more of a musical-note appearance. The cap is often drawn last, after the belly.

FAQ — 5 in Cursive

How do you write 5 in cursive?

Draw the rounded belly first (a clockwise arc from mid-left to the baseline), then add the cap stroke across the top. The two-part motion — belly then cap — is the standard cursive approach.

What does cursive 5 look like?

Cursive 5 has a rounded 'belly' curve at the bottom and a flat or slightly curved cap at the top, resembling a stylised musical note or a capital 'S' with a flat top.

Is 5 hard in cursive?

Moderately — the belly must be round and consistent, and placing the cap stroke correctly above it takes spatial awareness. Practice the belly arc first until it is smooth, then add the cap.

How does the cursive 5 cap stroke work?

The cap is drawn as the second stroke — a short horizontal (or slightly arched) line placed above the belly. It extends a bit past the belly on the right. In some handwriting styles, the cap stroke has a slight upward arch.
