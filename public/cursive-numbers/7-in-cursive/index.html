How to Write 7 (Seven) in Cursive

Begin with a horizontal cap stroke from left to right at the top. Without lifting the pen, pivot at the right end and draw a diagonal downstroke angling to the lower-left. Many cursive writers add a horizontal crossbar through the middle of the diagonal, especially to distinguish 7 from 1. The crossbar is drawn last — lift and cross.

Stroke Steps

  1. Draw a horizontal cap stroke left-to-right at the top
  2. At the right end, pivot and angle a diagonal downstroke to the lower-left
  3. Optionally: lift the pen and draw a short horizontal crossbar through the middle of the diagonal
  4. The crossbar helps distinguish 7 from 1 in fast cursive handwriting

7 in Print vs Cursive

Print 7 typically has no crossbar. In many cursive traditions — especially European — the crossbar is standard. Cursive 7 also has a slightly more angled, stylised diagonal compared to the straighter printed version.

FAQ — 7 in Cursive

How do you write 7 in cursive?

Draw a horizontal cap stroke left-to-right, then pivot and angle a diagonal downstroke to the lower-left. Optionally add a crossbar through the middle of the diagonal to distinguish it from 1.

Why do some cursive 7s have a crossbar?

The crossbar helps distinguish 7 from 1 in fast handwriting, especially in certain European handwriting traditions where a plain 1 might be mistaken for a 7 or vice versa. It is particularly common in French, German, and Scandinavian cursive styles.

What is the difference between cursive 7 and cursive 1?

Cursive 1 has a small upward entry stroke at the top-left. Cursive 7 has a full horizontal cap stroke across the top. Adding a crossbar to 7 makes the distinction even clearer.

Is 7 easy to write in cursive?

Yes — cursive 7 is one of the simpler digits. Two main strokes (cap + diagonal) plus an optional crossbar make it quick to write and easy to learn.
