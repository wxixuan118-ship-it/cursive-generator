How to Write 9 (Nine) in Cursive

Begin at the upper-right of the loop and draw a counterclockwise circle — forming a closed loop at the top. From the bottom of the loop, without lifting the pen, continue downward in a slightly curved tail. The tail can descend straight, curve gently to the left, or curl at the base depending on personal style.

Stroke Steps

  1. Start at the 2 o'clock position of the upper loop
  2. Draw counterclockwise: up and left, down the left side, right along the bottom
  3. Close the loop at the starting point
  4. Without lifting, continue the downward tail below the loop
  5. Optionally curl the tail slightly to the left at the base

9 in Print vs Cursive

Print 9 often has a straight vertical tail. Cursive 9 integrates the loop and tail into one fluid stroke, and the tail frequently has a slight leftward curve or curl rather than being perfectly straight.

FAQ — 9 in Cursive

How do you write 9 in cursive?

Draw a counterclockwise loop at the top, closing it, then continue downward into a slightly curved tail. The whole digit is one continuous stroke — loop and tail connect without lifting the pen.

What is the difference between cursive 9 and cursive 6?

Cursive 6 has its loop at the bottom with an open top. Cursive 9 has its loop at the top with a descending tail below. They mirror each other — 9 is an upside-down 6 in basic form.

Is 9 hard to write in cursive?

Not particularly. The loop-plus-tail structure is straightforward. The most common challenge is making the tail consistent — decide in advance whether yours will be straight, curved, or curled, and practise that version.

Should the tail of cursive 9 curl?

It is a matter of personal style and handwriting tradition. European cursive traditions often use a straight or slightly left-leaning tail. American cursive sometimes uses a curled or looped tail. Either is correct.
