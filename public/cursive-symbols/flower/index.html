Frequently Asked Questions

How do I copy a flower symbol?

Click any flower in the collection above — it copies to your clipboard instantly. Paste it into any app with Ctrl+V or Cmd+V.

What is the ✿ symbol?

✿ (U+273F) is called Black Florette and is a classic floral symbol used in typography and text decoration. It is simpler and more text-like than the flower emoji.

Can I use flower symbols in my Instagram bio?

Yes. Copy any flower, go to Instagram → Edit Profile → Bio, paste the symbol, and save.

What is the difference between ✿ and 🌸?

✿ is a plain Unicode text character that looks like a typographic flower ornament. 🌸 is an emoji that renders as a colourful cherry blossom on most platforms. Both copy and paste the same way.

Do flower symbols work on all phones?

Yes. The Unicode florette symbols (✿ ❀ ❁) and flower emoji (🌸 🌺 🌼) are supported across iOS and Android in all major apps.
