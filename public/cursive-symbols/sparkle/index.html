Frequently Asked Questions

How do I copy a sparkle symbol?

Click any sparkle above — it copies instantly to your clipboard. Paste with Ctrl+V or Cmd+V.

What is ✨ (sparkles)?

✨ (U+2728) is the Sparkles emoji, three four-pointed stars in a shimmering arrangement. It is one of the most popular symbols for aesthetic bios and captions.

What is ✦ (four-pointed star)?

✦ (U+2726) is the Black Four-Pointed Star, a plain text character (not an emoji) that looks like a diamond or sparkle shape. It is popular in aesthetic text and works in any app.

Can I use sparkle symbols in my TikTok name?

Yes. TikTok display names support Unicode. Paste ✨, ✦, or ✧ directly into your TikTok display name.

What is the difference between ✨ and ✦?

✨ is an emoji that renders as a colourful animated-style sparkle. ✦ is a plain Unicode text character — simpler and more consistent across different apps and fonts.
