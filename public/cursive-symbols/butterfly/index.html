Frequently Asked Questions

How do I copy a butterfly symbol?

Click any symbol above — it copies to your clipboard automatically. Paste with Ctrl+V or Cmd+V.

Is there a butterfly text symbol?

There is no plain text (non-emoji) butterfly character in Unicode. The closest is 🦋 (U+1F98B), the butterfly emoji. ❧ and ❦ are decorative floral symbols sometimes used as butterfly alternatives in aesthetic text.

Can I use 🦋 in my Instagram bio?

Yes. Copy 🦋 and paste it directly into your Instagram bio. Butterfly emoji are fully supported on iOS and Android.

What are aesthetic symbols for bios?

Popular aesthetic symbols include ✨🌙☽✿❀🌸♡★☆❧❦ and combinations of these. Many soft-aesthetic bios use a mix of moons, stars, flowers, and hearts.

Do these symbols work on TikTok?

Yes. TikTok supports Unicode emoji and symbols in display names and bios. Copy and paste any symbol from above directly into your TikTok profile.
