Frequently Asked Questions

How do I copy a moon symbol?

Click any moon in the collection above — it copies to your clipboard. Paste it with Ctrl+V or Cmd+V into any app.

What is ☽ (crescent moon)?

☽ (U+263D) is a classic Unicode crescent moon symbol, distinct from the emoji version 🌙. It is simpler and more like a typographic ornament, popular in aesthetic and mystical bios.

Can I use moon symbols on Instagram?

Yes. Copy any moon, open Instagram, go to Edit Profile → Bio or Name, paste the symbol, and save.

What is the difference between ☽ and 🌙?

☽ is a plain Unicode text character shaped like a classic crescent. 🌙 is an emoji that renders as a stylized yellow crescent on most platforms. Both copy and paste the same way.

Do moon symbols work on dark-mode profiles?

Yes. The text-based crescent symbols (☽ ☾) look especially elegant on dark-background profiles because they appear light on dark, matching the aesthetic.
