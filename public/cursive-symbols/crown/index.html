Frequently Asked Questions

How do I copy a crown symbol?

Click any crown in the collection above — it copies automatically. Paste with Ctrl+V or Cmd+V.

What is 👑 (crown emoji)?

👑 (U+1F451) is the Crown emoji, rendered as a yellow jewelled crown on most platforms. It is the most widely used crown symbol for bios and captions.

Can I use a crown in my Instagram username?

Instagram usernames only support letters, numbers, underscores, and periods — symbols are not allowed in usernames. However, you can add crown symbols to your display name (profile name) and bio.

What is ♛ (chess queen)?

♛ (U+265B) is the Black Chess Queen character — it looks like a queen crown piece and works well as a subtle crown symbol in text-based contexts.

Do crown symbols work on TikTok?

Yes. TikTok display names and bios support Unicode characters. Paste 👑 or ♛ directly into your TikTok profile.
