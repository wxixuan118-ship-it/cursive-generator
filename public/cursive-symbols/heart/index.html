Frequently Asked Questions

How do I copy a heart symbol?

Click the heart above — it copies to your clipboard automatically. Then paste it anywhere with Ctrl+V or Cmd+V.

Do heart symbols work on iPhone?

Yes. All symbols in this list are Unicode characters supported on iOS and Android. Paste them into any app including Instagram, TikTok, Messages, and WhatsApp.

What is the difference between ♥ and ❤?

♥ (U+2665) is the classic playing card suit heart. ❤ (U+2764) is the heavy black heart, which most platforms render as red. Both copy and paste the same way.

Can I put a heart in my Instagram bio?

Yes. Copy any heart from above, open Instagram, go to Edit Profile → Bio, paste the symbol, and save.

What is the cursive heart symbol?

The cursive heart is typically ❥ (U+2765), a rotated floral heart bullet that has a script, handwritten quality compared to the standard heart shapes.
