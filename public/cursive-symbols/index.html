How to Copy and Paste Unicode Symbols

Every symbol in these collections is a standard Unicode character that works on iOS, Android, Windows, and Mac. Click a symbol to copy it to your clipboard, then paste with Ctrl+V or Cmd+V.

Where can I use these symbols?

Unicode symbols work in Instagram bios and captions, TikTok display names and bios, Discord usernames and server names, Twitter/X bios, WhatsApp status, iMessage, and any website or app that renders Unicode text.

What is the difference between text symbols and emoji?

Text symbols (like ♥ ★ ✿ ☽) are plain Unicode characters. They always appear in black-and-white and render consistently in any font. Emoji (like ❤️ ⭐ 🌸 🌙) render in colour and may look slightly different across platforms, but carry more visual impact.
