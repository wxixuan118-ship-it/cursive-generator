Frequently Asked Questions

How do I copy an arrow symbol?

Click any arrow above — it copies to your clipboard. Paste with Ctrl+V or Cmd+V into any app.

What is the best arrow for Instagram bios?

→ (right arrow) and ➡ are both popular for structuring Instagram bios. Many people use → before each line item to create a clean list format.

Can I use arrows as bullet points?

Yes. Arrows like → ➜ ➡ work well as bullet points in social media bios and captions where traditional bullet characters may not format correctly.

What is the difference between → and ➡?

→ (U+2192) is a standard thin arrow. ➡ (U+27A1) is a heavier, bolder filled black arrow. Both copy and paste the same way.

Do arrow symbols work on all platforms?

Yes. Standard Unicode arrows are supported on iOS, Android, Windows, and Mac across all major apps including Instagram, TikTok, Discord, and WhatsApp.
