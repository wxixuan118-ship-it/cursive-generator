Which Cursive Style Is Best for Names?

For social media display names (Instagram, TikTok, Discord): Bold Cursive is the best choice. Its thicker strokes remain legible at the small sizes social platforms use for display names.

For invitations and formal cards: Cursive Script has thinner, more elegant strokes that look refined at the larger sizes used in print and digital cards.

For a dramatic or creative effect: Old Style Script uses Gothic-inspired letterforms that give any name a distinctive, artistic quality.

Why Do Names Look Good in Cursive?

Names are a natural fit for cursive because cursive's flowing, connected letterforms closely mimic the way people actually sign their names by hand. A name written in cursive carries visual associations of personalisation, formality, and identity that printed names do not. This is why cursive names are so popular in Instagram bios, wedding stationery, and personalised gift products.
