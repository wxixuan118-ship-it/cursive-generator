How to Copy and Paste Cursive Text

Type your text into the generator at the top of the page. The tool converts every letter into Unicode cursive characters in real time. Click the Copy button next to the style you want — or click any example in the grid above. Then paste the result into your target app: Instagram bio, TikTok display name, Discord username, WhatsApp message, Twitter/X profile, or any field that accepts Unicode text.

Why Unicode Cursive Works Everywhere

These are not images or font files — they are standard Unicode text characters from the mathematical script and Fraktur alphabets. Because they are plain text, they paste correctly into any app that supports Unicode, without needing to install anything. The characters display using each platform's built-in Unicode rendering, so they work on iOS, Android, Windows, and macOS.

Platform Compatibility

Cursive copy-paste text works in Instagram (bios, captions, display names), TikTok (bio and display name), Twitter/X (bio and name), Discord (username, bio, server name), LinkedIn (about section), WhatsApp and iMessage (messages), Snapchat (display name), and most web-based text fields. Always paste and preview in your target app before publishing to confirm correct display.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I copy a single cursive letter?

Click any cell in the alphabet table below — the cursive letter copies to your clipboard instantly. You can then paste it into Instagram, TikTok, Discord, or any other app that supports Unicode text.

What does cursive A look like in copy and paste?

In Unicode script, capital A looks like 𝒜 and lowercase a looks like 𝒶. In bold script, capital A is 𝓐 and lowercase a is 𝓪. In gothic blackletter, capital A is 𝔄 and lowercase a is 𝔞.

Are cursive capital letters different from lowercase cursive?

Yes. Cursive capital letters tend to be more elaborate and decorative than lowercase letters. In the Unicode script alphabet, capitals like 𝒜, ℬ, 𝒞 have more flourishes, while lowercase letters like 𝒶, 𝒷, 𝒸 are simpler and more flowing.

Can I combine cursive letters with regular text?

Yes. Because Unicode cursive characters are standard text, they can be mixed freely with regular letters, numbers, punctuation, and emoji. Many people use a single cursive initial or word within otherwise plain text for emphasis.

Do all letters have a cursive version?

All 26 letters of the Latin alphabet — both uppercase and lowercase — have Unicode mathematical script equivalents. Numbers and most punctuation remain unchanged, as Unicode math scripts only define letters.
