Adopt Me Fonts vs Roblox Username Rules

Roblox usernames and display names are restricted to letters, numbers and up to one underscore, so no font generator can style them — this page is for pet names, trade posts and bios instead. Simple bubbles, script and single hearts pass most reliably; long symbol chains are more likely to be filtered.

Adopt Me Font FAQ

Can I change my Roblox username font with this?

No. Roblox usernames and display names only allow letters, numbers and underscores. These fonts are for pet names, trade posts, bios and Discord.

Yes, pet names accept Unicode text, though they pass through the Roblox chat filter. Simple bubble letters and script with a single heart pass most reliably.

The Roblox filter replaced characters it did not recognise, which is more common on accounts under 13. Try a plainer style or fewer symbols.
