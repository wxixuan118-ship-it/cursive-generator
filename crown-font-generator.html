Crown Font Generator vs Royal Frame ꧁꧂

People search for a crown font and often mean the ꧁꧂ frame, so this page includes both. The chess crowns and 👑 are literal crowns placed before and after the name. The ꧁꧂ frame is not a crown at all but two Javanese punctuation marks whose curled shape reads as wings or a crest; it became the standard gaming frame because it renders in almost every game font. The ♔ ♕ ♚ ♛ crowns are chess symbols from the Miscellaneous Symbols block of the Unicode Standard, which is why they render as plain text on every platform without an emoji font.

Which Crown Font Renders in Games

Game name fields are the strictest place a crown font generator result will go, and the four crown types behave differently there. The chess crowns ♛ ♕ ♚ ♔ are in the basic Miscellaneous Symbols block, so nearly every game font includes them; they are the safest choice. The ꧁꧂ frame is from the Javanese block, which most mobile games render but some console and PC games show as boxes. The 👑 emoji depends on the game shipping an emoji font, which many do not.

Lettering matters as much as the crown. Gothic (Fraktur) and bold cursive letters come from the Mathematical Alphanumeric Symbols block, which Free Fire, PUBG Mobile and most Unity-based mobile games render fine, while a few older titles show the letters but drop the crown or the reverse. Bold sans and small caps are the most conservative pairings. If a decorated name is rejected, the usual fix is to keep the crown and switch the letters to plain text, or keep the fancy letters and drop to a single ♛.

Outside games, none of this applies: Discord, Instagram, TikTok and WhatsApp render every row on this page. The one practical limit is length. A ꧁꧂ frame plus a ♛ crown plus gothic letters can push a 16-character game name over its limit because some crowns count as two characters, so preview the exact name and trim before you copy.

Crown Font FAQ

What is a crown font?

Online, a crown font means text decorated with crown symbols, such as ♛ 𝓠𝓾𝓮𝓮𝓷 ♛ or 👑 Name 👑. It is Unicode text you can copy and paste, not an installed typeface.

Is this crown font generator free?

Yes. It runs in your browser, needs no account or download, and every result can be copied without limits.

How do I put a crown in my Free Fire name?

Type your name, copy a ♛ or ꧁꧂ row, open your Free Fire profile, paste it into the name field and save. Chess crowns render most reliably in game fonts.

What is the ꧁꧂ symbol?

It is a pair of Javanese punctuation marks. Gamers call it the crown or wing frame because of its shape, and it renders in most mobile game fonts.

Why does the crown show as a box?

The app is missing a glyph for that character, most often the 👑 emoji or the ꧁꧂ frame in a game. Switch to a ♛ or ♔ row, which uses the most widely supported crown.

Can I use a crown in my Instagram username?

Not in the @username, which only allows letters, numbers, periods and underscores. Display names, bios and captions accept crowns.
