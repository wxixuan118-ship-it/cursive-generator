How to Write Costa Rica in Cursive

Costa Rica is the only Central American country name on this site written as two words, which changes how you plan it on the page. Write the capital C as a single open curve that sweeps in from the top and finishes at the baseline. The cursive C connects, so continue directly into the o.

The o closes at the top, which means the next letter, s, starts from a bridge stroke at the midline rather than from the baseline. Then form the t with a short ascender, join to the a, and let the a finish with a longer exit stroke than usual. That extended tail becomes the visual gap between the two words. A space in cursive should be about the width of one lowercase letter, wider than a join but narrower than a printed space.

Lift the pen and begin the capital R. The cursive R has a stem, a bowl, and a leg that lands on the baseline, and from that leg it joins into the i. Continue through the c and the final a. Cross the t and dot the i last so both words share the same baseline and slant. Check that the two capitals are the same height; uneven capitals are the most common flaw in a two-word cursive title.

Costa Rica, Pura Vida, and Accents

Pura vida is the phrase most people want next to Costa Rica. It means "pure life" and works as a greeting, a farewell, and a way of saying that everything is fine. Both words use plain letters, so the generator converts the whole phrase in every style. The same is true for Costa Rica itself: the country name has no accents, unlike many Spanish place names.

The name means "rich coast", and the Spanish spelling matches the English one exactly, so a bilingual design can use one cursive version for both audiences. If you add a town name such as San José, the accented é will stay plain, which is worth knowing before you build a heading around it.

Costa Rica in Cursive FAQ

How wide should the space be between Costa and Rica in cursive?

About the width of one lowercase letter. Extend the exit stroke of the final a in Costa, lift the pen, and start the capital R after a gap that is clearly wider than a join but narrower than a printed space.

Does the capital R in Rica connect to the i?

Yes. The cursive capital R ends with a leg that lands on the baseline, and that leg leads straight into the lowercase i.

Can I write Pura Vida in cursive with this tool?

Yes. Pura Vida contains only plain letters, so both words convert fully in the elegant, bold, and decorative styles.

Is Costa Rica spelled the same in Spanish and English?

Yes. The spelling is identical and has no accents, so one cursive version serves both languages.
