    How Our PUBG Username Generator Works

    There is no AI behind this page and no connection to PUBG or Krafton. PUBG usernames are assembled in your browser from five ingredients:

    • Your word. The name or word you type is cleaned to letters and numbers and kept whole in every result. Leave it blank and the generator picks a PUBG noun instead.
    • PUBG vocabulary. Each of the eight styles has its own hand-written list — sniper, fragger and IGL for pro; AWM, Kar98 and headshot for roles; king, raja and bhai for the BGMI classics — so a funny result never borrows a pro word by accident.
    • Lobby patterns. Adjective + name, name + op, clan tag + name, name + gun, king + name, underscore joins and reference numbers. Patterns rotate within a batch so you never get 24 versions of the same idea.
    • Length awareness. The Short filter keeps results at 8 characters or fewer, leaving room for a clan tag or a symbol pair inside PUBG Mobile's 14-character limit.
    • Favorites. The heart saves a name to your browser's localStorage. Nothing is sent to a server, and the generator cannot check whether a name is free — the rename screen tells you when you try.

    PUBG Username Rules and Rename Cards

    PUBG Mobile and BGMI names are capped at 14 characters and can include letters, numbers, underscores and a wide range of Unicode symbols — which is why lobbies are full of ꧁☬ brackets and small-text letters. Changing your name costs a Rename Card: you get one free at level 10, and after that they come from the shop, events or the Royale Pass, so most players change their name rarely and should test it here first. PUBG: Battlegrounds on PC and console is stricter — 4 to 16 characters, letters, numbers, underscores and hyphens only, no symbols — and PUBG New State allows 2 to 16 characters.

    That is why this PUBG username generator keeps symbols off by default and offers a Short filter: an 8-character plain name leaves six characters for a clan tag or a symbol pair, works on every PUBG version, and can be typed by a friend sending a request. Style only the display version.     PUBG Username Generator FAQ

    What is a PUBG username generator?

    It is a tool that combines a word you choose with curated PUBG vocabulary — sniper, fragger, AWM, clan tags, king and boss — and lobby naming patterns to produce PUBG Mobile, BGMI and PC name ideas within the character limits. This one runs in your browser, is free, and lets you copy any result with one click.

    What are some stylish PUBG username ideas?

    Examples from the styles above: prosniper, silentawm, txsquad, hollowvoid, noobwithpan, kingarjun, ironwolf and neonvortex. Type your own name to get versions built around it, such as proarjun or arjunop, then style it with Unicode letters if you want the decorated look.

    How many characters can a PUBG username have?

    PUBG Mobile and BGMI allow up to 14 characters including symbols. PUBG: Battlegrounds on PC allows 4–16 characters with letters, numbers, underscores and hyphens only. PUBG New State allows 2–16. Use the Short filter to leave room for a clan tag or symbols.

    How do I change my PUBG username?

    In PUBG Mobile or BGMI, go to Settings → Basic → Rename and use a Rename Card; you get one free at level 10, and later ones come from the shop, events or the Royale Pass. Because cards are limited, test the name here and copy it exactly before you spend one.

    Can I use symbols in my PUBG username?

    Yes on PUBG Mobile, BGMI and New State — ꧁, ☬, ★ and small-text Unicode letters all work in the name field. No on PC and console. Every symbol counts toward the 14-character limit, so ꧁☬name☬꧂ leaves only 8 characters for the name itself.

    Does this PUBG username generator check availability?

    No. Nothing here talks to PUBG, Krafton or Tencent, so it cannot know whether a name is taken. Copy the one you like and try it on the rename screen — it tells you immediately if the name is unavailable, before the Rename Card is spent.

    What is a good PUBG squad or clan username format?

    A two-letter clan tag in front of each member's name — TXArjun, TXSam, TXNova — is the standard BGMI format. Pick the Squad & Clan style above to generate tags, or take one base and change only the name after the tag for each player.

    Can I copy and paste the generated PUBG usernames?

    Yes. Every result has a Copy button, Copy All Usernames copies the visible list one per line, and the heart saves a name to My Favorites in your browser. For the decorated look, paste the plain name into the Free Fire font generator.
