Copy & Paste Cursive Tools Cursive Generator

The generator makes two kinds of cursive signature. The signature maker at the top sets your name in real script fonts, or records one you draw, and saves it as an image: a transparent PNG or a scalable SVG. The copy-and-paste section underneath converts your name into Unicode script characters instead, which stay text and paste into any field that accepts text. Use the image for documents and email, and the text for social profiles.

Signature Maker for Email, PDFs and Documents

The most common use is an email signature: download the PNG, open the signature settings in Gmail, Outlook or Apple Mail, insert the image above your name and title, and keep it around 300–400 pixels wide so it does not dominate the footer. For a PDF you sign yourself, such as a form, a cover letter or an invoice, insert the PNG as an image in the PDF editor, or in Word or Google Docs before exporting. Designers use the SVG: it embeds the font, so the signature stays sharp at any size and can be recoloured in Illustrator, Figma or Canva for a logo, a book plate or a watermark.

An image of a signature is not the same as a legal e-signature. In the United States, the Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act recognises electronic signatures where there is intent to sign and consent to do business electronically, which is what dedicated e-signature services record. Use this tool for footers, self-signed PDFs, print and design; use an e-signature service for contracts.

Signature Fonts in the Generator

The typed signatures use eight script fonts, all under the SIL Open Font License, so the downloads are free for personal and commercial use. CTG Everly Script, a light natural hand made for this site, is the default, with swash-underline, slanted and compact versions. CTG Signature Script is a heavier marker-pen hand. Sacramento gives a thin monoline signature, Mrs Saint Delafield and Herr Von Muellerhoff a fast, slanted executive scrawl, Alex Brush a brush-pen look, and Great Vibes and Allura a formal, looping script. Six ink colours cover black, blue and navy for documents and red, green and sepia for creative work, and three pen weights change the stroke from a fine liner to a felt tip.

The font size slider runs from 24 to 72 pixels; the preview shows the real size, and the PNG is exported at three times that, so at the default 48px a signature downloads roughly 190 to 250 pixels tall depending on the font. For an email footer, pick a smaller size; for print or a logo, go larger or use the SVG.

Typed Signature vs Drawn Signature

Choose typed when you want something consistent and presentable in seconds: a professional email footer, a name on a certificate, a signature line for a newsletter. Choose drawn when the signature has to be yours, such as a letter, a card or a document where a font would look impersonal, or when you want a mark nobody else can reproduce by typing the same name. The draw pad works with a mouse, a finger on a phone or a stylus on a tablet, smooths the stroke as you go, and trims the download to the signature itself. Either way, the file stays on your device.

Cursive Signature Styles Explained

Elegant: Clean Unicode script and italic styles without extra decoration — ideal for display names where readability matters.
Fancy: Names wrapped in decorative frames, brackets, and border symbols like ꧁꧂ and 【】 — popular for gaming usernames and profile names.
Bold: Thick, high-visibility bold cursive — best for Instagram names and anywhere small text needs to stand out on mobile.
Cute: Soft, kawaii-style decoration with flowers, glitter, and gentle symbols — popular on TikTok bios and WhatsApp.
Heart: Heart symbols (♡ ❤ 💕 ❣) flanking your name — the most widely requested signature style for Instagram bios.
Symbols: Star trails, sparkles, arrows, and ornamental symbol combinations for creative profiles.

Fancy vs. Elegant Cursive Signatures — Which One to Choose

The two most requested looks pull in opposite directions. An elegant cursive signature is the bare letterforms — Cursive Script (𝒮𝑜𝓅𝒽𝒾𝒶), Bold Cursive (𝓢𝓸𝓹𝓱𝓲𝓪) or Bold Italic — with nothing around them. It reads as a real signature because that is what a real signature looks like: no frame, no symbols, just a name with a consistent slant. Pick it for email sign-offs, LinkedIn headlines, a wedding website, or any bio where you want people to read the name before they notice the styling.

A fancy cursive signature keeps the same letters but adds a frame: ꧁𝓢𝓸𝓹𝓱𝓲𝓪꧂, ✧･ﾟ 𝒮𝑜𝓅𝒽𝒾𝒶 ･ﾟ✧, or ★彡 𝓢𝓸𝓹𝓱𝓲𝓪 彡★. The frame is what people mean by "fancy" — it makes a short name fill a display-name field and stand out in a comment thread. The cost is length (the ꧁꧂ brackets alone are four characters) and legibility on small screens, so use it for gaming tags, TikTok and Discord display names, and keep the elegant version for anything printed or professional.

A quick way to decide: if the signature will sit next to your real name or a photo, go elegant; if it has to do the job of a logo on its own, go fancy. Our guide to cursive signature styles goes deeper on choosing one. Both live under the Elegant and Fancy tabs above, and the Signature Builder lets you start from an elegant style and add a single symbol on one side — usually the best compromise.

Cursive Signature for Instagram Bio

Instagram supports Unicode text in both the Name and Bio fields. The Name field (which appears in bold under your username and in search results) has a 30-character limit — choose a shorter signature style. The Bio field allows 150 characters — enough for a signature with symbols. Bold Cursive is the most readable on small mobile screens; heart signatures are the most popular for personal accounts.

Cursive Signature Text vs. a Real Handwritten Signature

Unicode cursive signature text is decorative — it looks like a cursive signature and works for social media profiles, bios, display names, usernames, and personal decoration. It is not:

  • A legal electronic signature for contracts or documents
  • A cryptographically verified signature
  • A replacement for a bank or government-accepted signature

For Instagram, TikTok, Discord and WhatsApp, Unicode cursive signature text works well. For an email footer, a printed letter or a PDF, use the PNG or SVG from the signature maker instead, since it looks the same on every device.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this cursive signature generator free?

Yes. There is no account, watermark, download limit or upload. Everything runs in your browser, and your name or drawing never leaves your device.

How do I make a cursive signature image?

Type your name in the signature maker at the top of the page, pick a font size, ink colour and pen weight, then click PNG or SVG on the style you like. To sign by hand instead, open the Draw tab, sign with your mouse, finger or stylus and click Download PNG.

Can I use the signature in Gmail or Outlook?

Yes. Download the PNG, then insert it as an image in your email signature settings. The transparent background sits cleanly on white or coloured themes. Keep it around 300 to 400 pixels wide.

Is a generated signature legally binding?

An image of a signature is not by itself a legal electronic signature. Laws such as the US ESIGN Act recognise e-signatures when there is intent to sign and a record of consent, which is what e-signature services provide. Use this tool for email footers, documents you print, PDFs you sign yourself and design work; use an e-signature service for contracts.

What is the difference between PNG and SVG?

PNG is a pixel image at three times the preview size with a transparent background, ready for email, Word, Google Docs and PDFs; a larger font size gives a larger image. SVG is a vector file with the font embedded, so it scales to any size without blurring and can be edited in design apps.

Can I copy and paste my cursive signature as text?

Yes. The copy-and-paste section below the signature maker turns your name into 40+ Unicode cursive styles. Click Copy and paste with Ctrl+V, Cmd+V or long-press Paste on mobile. These are characters, not an image, so they work in bios and messages but do not use the signature fonts.

Can I use a cursive signature on Instagram?

Yes, as text. Copy a Bold Cursive or Script style from the copy-and-paste section, then go to Instagram, Edit Profile, Name or Bio, and paste it. Images cannot go in those fields, so use the text styles there.

Why does my text signature look different on some devices?

Older devices may not support the full Unicode script range. Switch to Bold Cursive, which has the broadest support across modern iOS and Android. The PNG and SVG signatures look the same everywhere because they are images.
