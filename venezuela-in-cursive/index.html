How to Write Venezuela in Cursive

Start with the capital V. Unlike most cursive capitals, the V finishes at the top of the letter, so if it joins the next letter at all it does so with a short bridge stroke at the midline, the same way a lowercase o or w joins. Many school styles treat V as a non-connecting capital and lift the pen instead. Either way, the lowercase e starts at the midline, not the baseline.

The middle section, e-n-e-z-u-e, contains three e's. Keep each one the same size, a small closed loop. The n has two humps. The z starts with a small hump at the midline, loops below the baseline, and comes back up to join the u; think of it as a y with a wavy top. Give it the same descender depth you would use for a g.

Finish with l-a. The l is a tall loop that reaches full ascender height, and it is the only tall lowercase stroke in the word, so it balances the capital V at the start. End the a with a short tail. With nine letters and only one descender, Venezuela stays mostly in the midline band, which makes even letter heights especially important.

The Name Venezuela and Its Meaning

Venezuela is usually explained as "little Venice", a name given by early European explorers who saw stilt houses on Lake Maracaibo and were reminded of the Italian city. That link gives designers a natural pairing: the Italian word Venezia and the country name share the same first four letters and the same looped cursive z.

The Spanish spelling of the country is identical to the English one and carries no accent, so one cursive version serves both languages. The demonym venezolano and the capital Caracas also convert fully. Only phrases with accents, such as Día de la Independencia, will show a plain letter in the middle.

Venezuela in Cursive FAQ

Does the capital V in Venezuela connect to the e?

Only from the top. The cursive capital V finishes at the top of the letter, so it either joins the e with a short bridge stroke at the midline or, in many school styles, does not join at all and the pen is lifted.

How do you write the z in Venezuela in cursive?

Start with a small hump at the midline, drop into a loop below the baseline, and return to the baseline to join the u. It resembles a y with a wavy top and should match the depth of your other descenders.

Is Venezuela spelled the same in Spanish?

Yes. The Spanish and English spellings are identical and have no accent, so one cursive version works for both.

Why is Venezuela called little Venice?

Early European explorers saw houses on stilts around Lake Maracaibo and were reminded of Venice, so the region was named after the Italian city. The names share their first four letters.
