Reporting a Display Problem

If a cursive style shows as boxes, question marks or the wrong characters, include the page URL, the style name, your device and operating system version, and the app where you pasted the text. A screenshot helps a lot. Our compatibility guide and the article on why cursive text shows as boxes cover the most common causes.
