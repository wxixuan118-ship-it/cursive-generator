Text Daisies vs the Emoji Daisy Font

The daisy is the one flower with good monochrome text options, and a daisy font generator can use them where emoji fail. The ❁ florette (U+2741) has eight outlined petals around a dark centre and reads as a daisy at small sizes; the ⚘ flower (U+2698) is a single bloom on a stem and reads as a sprig. Both come from blocks that ship in every system font, so they render in game name fields, older forums and anywhere else that shows a 🌼 as a box.

The 🌼 blossom emoji is the colour option. It is yellow and white on every platform but drawn slightly differently on iOS, Android and Windows, and it counts as two characters in some length limits. In a display name it pops; in a subtitle or a caption the text daisies are often the better fit because they inherit the text colour and stay small.

Lettering follows the frame. Cursive script keeps the daisy delicate; bold cursive stands up to the emoji in a display name; circled letters with a ꒰ ꒱ curl or ⑅ petal wrap give the kawaii sticker look; italic with a ✿ drifts toward cottagecore. Preview the exact name you plan to use, and if you are pasting into a place with strict rules, start with a ❁ row.

Daisy Font FAQ

What is a daisy font?

Online, a daisy font means text decorated with daisy symbols or the 🌼 emoji, such as 🌼 𝒩𝒶𝓂𝑒 🌼 or ❁ Name ❁. It is Unicode text you can copy and paste, not an installed typeface.

Is this daisy font generator free?

Yes. It runs in your browser, needs no account or download, and every result can be copied without limits.

Is there a text-only daisy symbol?

Yes. The ❁ florette and the ⚘ flower sprig are monochrome Unicode characters that read as daisies and render in every font. Both are on this page.

Can I use daisy text on TikTok?

Yes. Paste it into your display name, bio or caption. TikTok shows both the 🌼 emoji and the text daisies.

Why does the daisy look different on my friend's phone?

Emoji are drawn by each platform, so the 🌼 on iOS, Android and Windows are different illustrations. The ❁ and ⚘ text daisies look the same everywhere.

Do daisies work in usernames?

Rarely. Most platforms restrict @usernames to letters, numbers and underscores. Use the display name, nickname or bio field instead.
